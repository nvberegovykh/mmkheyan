Data Privacy

This website is the official online gallery of artist Meruzhan Mkheyan, showcasing paintings and sculptures. We respect your privacy and keep the data we collect to a minimum.

What we collect

  • Basic, anonymized analytics (page views, approximate location, device and browser type) used only to understand how visitors browse the gallery and to improve the site.
  • No account registration, payment details, or personal contact information is collected anywhere on this site.

Cookies

The site may use minimal, essential cookies or local storage to remember your language preference. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

How we use data

Analytics data is used solely to operate, maintain, and improve the gallery experience. We do not sell, rent, or share visitor data with third parties.

Third-party services

Images are delivered via a third-party content hosting provider and basic site infrastructure (hosting, content delivery, security). These providers may process standard technical logs (such as IP address) as part of delivering the site to you.

Your rights

You may request information about, or deletion of, any data associated with your visit by writing to the gallery's site administrator through the channel listed on the artist's official pages.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the site. The current version always applies.
